System overview

Mentcare is a patient information system that supports the care of people with mental health problems across the health authority's day clinics. It has two goals: to give clinical staff a better record of each patient's diagnoses, treatments, and consultations, and to give health service managers better information about mental health care in the region.

Clinicians use Mentcare during consultations to read and update patient records, prescribe medication with safety checks, and record risk. Clinic administrators manage attendance, repeat prescriptions, staff meetings, and management reports. The Mental Health Act administrator tracks patients detained without consent and makes sure legally required reviews and letters happen on time. The medical records office controls access, handles patients' requests about their data, and produces anonymised reports for research.

Mentcare is not a complete medical record; it holds only mental health information. It works with the health authority's existing systems: single sign-on for authentication, the drug information system for drug details and costs, the PRESCRIPTION system for printing prescriptions, the APPOINTMENTS system for bookings, and the national summary patient record.

Mentcare is built in blocks by five teams and assembled into one system at the end of the semester. Team 4 builds the Admissions and Outreach block: referrals, triage, admission with a first appointment, and outreach to patients and carers. This guide describes the whole system from every user's point of view; screens 19 to 21 belong to the Team 4 block, which has its own detailed guide.

Screens in this guide are mockups of the planned product; all names and figures are sample data. The numbered markers on each screen match the numbered steps below it.

Working with Mentcare

Mentcare runs in the Firefox web browser and is available during clinic hours, 08:00 to 18:30 Monday to Friday; planned maintenance happens in the evenings and on Sunday mornings. Wherever possible, choices are made from menus of allowed values, such as the list of known conditions and the approved medication list. Typed input is checked, and if something is rejected the screen explains why. Free-text fields show how many characters are left. Queries about a patient normally respond within two seconds; if one takes longer, a progress message is shown. Forms are filled in with information Mentcare already holds so staff do not have to type it again.

All staff

Every user signs in through the health authority's single sign-on and can search for patients, subject to the permissions of their role.

Clinical staff

Doctors, nurses and other clinicians use Mentcare during consultations to read and update patient records. Outside consultations they can open records in read-only mode.

Figure 4. Record a consultation.

  1. Check the staff present. You are added automatically; add up to five more colleagues.

Clinic administrators

Administrators manage appointments and attendance, prepare repeat prescriptions, set up meetings about patients, and produce management reports. They see personal details such as name and address but not clinical details like diagnoses.

Mental Health Act administrator
